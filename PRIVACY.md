# Privacy Policy — Blur for WhatsApp Web

_Last updated: 21 August 2026_

**This extension does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.**

## What it does with your data

Nothing leaves your machine. The extension has no server, makes no network
requests, and contains no analytics, telemetry, tracking, or advertising code.

To blur what is on screen it reads the page structure of `web.whatsapp.com`
in your browser — the position of chat rows and message bubbles — and adds a
CSS blur to them. That reading happens entirely inside your own browser tab
and is never recorded, copied, or sent anywhere. Your messages, contact names,
phone numbers and profile photos are never accessed as data, stored, or
transmitted.

## What is saved

Your settings only — whether blurring is on, the blur strength, whether to
reveal on hover or click, and which areas to blur. These are kept in Chrome's
`storage.sync`, which means Chrome itself may sync them between your own
signed-in Chrome installations. They contain no personal information and no
message content.

## Permissions

- **storage** — saves the settings listed above.
- **Access to `web.whatsapp.com`** — required to apply the blur on that site.
  The extension runs on no other site.
